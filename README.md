# Healthcare-Data-Insight-and-Recommendation

### Project Overview
This project analyzes hospital patient data to understand the patient journey, length of stay, readmission, mortality, and discharge pathways.


## Business Problem

Hospitals generate large amounts of patient information every day. This information may include admission records, patient demographics, diagnoses, departments, admission dates, discharge dates, readmission status, and patient outcomes.

However, raw data does not immediately provide hospital management with a clear understanding of patient flow and performance.

The hospital may need answers to questions such as:

How many patients were admitted?
Which departments handled the highest number of patients?
What are the most common diagnoses?
What is the average length of stay?
Which departments have longer patient stays?
How many patients were readmitted?
What is the recorded mortality rate?
What are the most common discharge pathways?
Which age groups account for the largest number of admissions?
Are there differences between emergency and elective admissions?
Which diagnoses are associated with longer stays?
Which areas require further investigation?

The purpose of this project is to transform the available hospital data into clear metrics, visualizations, and insights that make these questions easier to answer.

## Objectives

The main objective of this project is to use data analytics to understand hospital operations and patient outcomes.

The specific objectives are to:

Analyze the patient journey from admission to discharge.
Measure and analyze Length of Stay (LOS).
Identify patterns in hospital readmissions.
Analyze recorded mortality patterns.
Examine different discharge pathways.
Compare patient outcomes across departments.
Analyze differences between admission types.
Identify patterns across age groups and diagnoses.
Develop meaningful hospital Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for exploring the data.
Generate data-driven insights that can support further operational investigation.

## Tool & Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel – data cleaning, formulas, pivot tables, and initial analysis
Power Query – data transformation and cleaning
Power BI – interactive dashboard and visualization
DAX – calculated measures and KPIs

## Key Takeaway

Department Performance
General Medicine recorded the highest patient volume, with 152 patients, followed by Emergency with 146 patients. General Medicine also generated the highest departmental revenue.

Patient Outcomes
The largest patient outcome categories were Recovered and Improved.

Recovered: 249 patients
Improved: 235 patients
Referred: 121 patients
Deceased: 107 patients
Top Diagnoses
Pneumonia was the most frequently recorded diagnosis, followed by Hypertension. Other common diagnoses included malaria, asthma, and diabetes-related conditions.

Operational Insights
The analysis identified patient waiting time and unpaid or pending payments as areas requiring management attention. Monthly admission fluctuations may also have implications for staffing, bed capacity, and resource planning.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how hospital data can be transformed into meaningful insights about the patient journey, Length of Stay, readmission, mortality, and discharge pathways.

The analysis provides a clearer view of patient flow and hospital outcomes while identifying areas that may require further investigation.

Overall, the project demonstrates the value of data-driven decision-making in healthcare, while recognizing the importance of data quality, patient privacy, and clinical context.

## Recommendation

Monitor Length of Stay by department and diagnosis to identify prolonged stays and potential delays.
Track readmissions and investigate patterns by diagnosis, age group, and discharge pathway.
Monitor mortality patterns while considering patient severity and clinical factors.
Improve discharge planning to support timely and appropriate patient transitions.
Monitor monthly admission trends to improve staffing and capacity planning
Track common diagnoses such as hypertension and pneumonia.
